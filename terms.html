1. Scope & Enrollment

1.1 Acceptance of Terms: By scheduling an assessment, booking a complimentary trial session, or completing enrollment in any Quran recitation, Tajweed, or classical Arabic course provided by Al-Mulk Islamische Akademie, you expressly enter into a legally binding contract under these operational terms.

1.2 Registration & Minor Eligibility: Courses are accessible to adult learners and minor students aged five (5) and above. Where enrollment pertains to a minor, the parent or appointed legal guardian confirms having legal authority to bind the student and agrees to ensure punctual attendance, accurate identity disclosure, and a quiet home setting conducive to recitation.

2. One-to-One Tuition & Scheduling

2.1 Live Zoom Classroom: All academic instruction is administered in real time through dedicated, encrypted one-to-one video meeting rooms. Links are generated securely within the student portal to safeguard personal contact information.

2.2 Punctuality Window: Lessons initiate precisely at the contracted start time. The tutor remains present and waiting in the digital classroom for fifteen (15) minutes past the hour. If the learner does not attend within this window, the lesson is marked as a completed session and cannot be reclaimed.

2.3 Audio & Environmental Safeguards: Because Tajweed and Arabic phonetics require rigorous vocal precision (Makharij al-Huruf), students must connect with a functioning microphone, clear audio, and positioned inside a quiet room free of disruptive background audio.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellations

3.1 12-Hour Prior Notice: We acknowledge that household and work responsibilities fluctuate. A scheduled session may be rescheduled at no additional fee provided written notice or calendar adjustment is executed via the student portal or verified WhatsApp support at least twelve (12) hours prior to the session start.

3.2 Late Cancellations & Missed Lessons: Notifications delivered with fewer than twelve (12) hours notice forfeit the reserved lesson credit. This protects our teachers, who dedicate dedicated preparation time and reserve calendar availability exclusively for each individual student.

3.3 Academy Absences & Substitute Coverage: If an instructor experiences unforeseen illness or connectivity disruption, the Academy will notify you promptly. You retain the choice between rescheduling the class at your preferred hour or receiving instruction from an equally certified, accredited substitute tutor.

4. Subscriptions & Lesson Packages

4.1 Recurring Monthly Membership: Subscriptions renew automatically every four (4) weeks. This modality locks your fixed recurring weekly time-slots with your preferred tutor. Pausing or canceling can be performed smoothly at any point before the onset of the next renewal cycle.

4.2 Prepaid Lesson Packages: Individual prepaid block packages carry a validity duration of six (6) full calendar months from date of purchase. Package credits offer maximum flexibility, allowing booking across varying days and transferable balance sharing across immediate family members.

4.3 Onboarding & Placement Fee: A singular €20 enrollment fee accompanies new recurring student subscriptions. This directly funds diagnostic Tajweed level assessment, student file creation, and tailored curriculum material distribution.

PEDAGOGICAL COMMITMENT

5. The Sacred Trust (Amanah)

Teaching the Book of Allah is not merely an academic endeavor; it is an enduring religious obligation and trust (Amanah) bestowed upon our institution. We hold our pedagogical team to uncompromising Islamic moral ethics: absolute gentleness, steadfast patience, genuine encouragement, and the complete elimination of harsh correction or humiliation. Every adult learner and young child is nurtured with the dignity, warmth, and respect appropriate to the sacred text.

6. Code of Conduct & Child Safeguarding

6.1 Child Protection Protocol: Al-Mulk Academy maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding any behavior compromising minor student well-being. Parents retain open authorization to unobtrusively observe live sessions at any moment without prior notification.

6.2 Mutual Adab & Respect: All students, guardians, and teaching personnel commit to communicating through exemplary Islamic etiquette (Adab). Defamatory conduct, abusive terminology, or inappropriate digital communications will result in instantaneous termination of services without recourse.

7. Termination & Policy Updates

7.1 Voluntary Termination: Monthly subscription models can be terminated by the student or guardian at any interval prior to the automated billing invoice. Remaining hours within active prepaid packages remain redeemable through their standard 6-month validity window.

7.2 Amendments: Al-Mulk Academy reserves the prerogative to revise operational terms to reflect statutory directives or curriculum evolutions. Registered patrons will receive written electronic notice thirty (30) days prior to the enforcement of substantive adjustments.

All proprietary workbooks, digital Tajweed charts, exercise sheets, and recorded webinars distributed by Al-Mulk Islamische Akademie are licensed strictly for private household usage.
