Our name

“Blessed is He in whose hand is the dominion.”

(Surah Al-Mulk 67:1)

Surah Al-Mulk is the surah the Prophet ﷺ described as interceding for the one who recites it until they are forgiven. We chose its name as a daily reminder of why we teach: so that every student can recite the Quran correctly, understand its profound message, and carry it lovingly through every chapter of life.

Bridging the gap

Learning the Quran shouldn't
depend on where you live.

For diaspora families, working professionals, and busy parents, locating a patient, certified Quran tutor nearby can feel overwhelming. Commutes take hours, group classes overlook individual struggle, and rigid weekend hours rarely align with real household demands.

Every learner deserves a sanctuary of respectful, patient 1:1 attention without geographic compromises.

No qualified teacher nearby

Many towns and suburbs lack verified scholars with verified chains of transmission (Ijaza) who are also fluent in English or Spanish.

Group classes that move too fast or too slow

In classrooms with 15 children or adults, quiet students slip behind, and advanced students grow bored. One-to-one is the traditional gold standard.

Schedules that don't fit work, school, or family

Rigid local madrasah hours clash with sports, overtime, and dinner. Quranic education must harmonize with your life, not cause friction.
