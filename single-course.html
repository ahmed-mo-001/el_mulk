Course Overview

Master recitation at your own pace

It is never too late to recite the words of Allah with complete confidence, rhythmic poise, and phonetic accuracy. Designed exclusively for adults balancing demanding careers, family life, and personal commitments, our Quran One-to-One program pairs you with a compassionate, certified scholar who honors your individual learning speed.

Whether you are discovering the Arabic alphabet for the very first time, correcting longstanding pronunciation hesitations, or seeking formal Ijaza-level Tajweed precision, your teacher crafts every session entirely around your goals in a private, supportive, zero-pressure environment.

MILESTONES

Course structure & progression

1

Level Check in Your Free Trial

Session 1

30-minute diagnostic session with your dedicated scholar. We assess your starting comfort level, pronunciation baseline, and build an adaptable customized learning plan.

2

Letters & Vowels (Harakat)

Weeks 2–6

Hands-on mastery of letter recognition, sound articulators (Makharij), short and long vowels, Tanween, and Sukoon using the acclaimed Noorani Qaida methodology.

3

Reading Verses with Tajweed

Weeks 7–16

Transitioning from individual words to complete Ayat. Integrating Madd prolongation, Ghunnah nasalization, heavy/light letters, and safe punctuation pauses (Waqf).

4

Fluent Reading of the Mushaf

Ongoing Mastery

Continuous recitation directly from the holy Uthmani script. Transition from assisted guidance to independent, melodious, and deeply confident Quranic reading.
